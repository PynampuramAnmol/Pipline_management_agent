title: Investigating missing tables and views
---
For TABLE_OR_VIEW_NOT_FOUND errors, check that the table name is spelled correctly and fully qualified as catalog.schema.table.
Run SHOW TABLES IN catalog.schema to confirm the table exists.
Check that the upstream job that creates this table ran successfully before this run.
Look for stray or unfinished code in the notebook, such as text left after the last cell, that stops the table from being created.
